## **Welcome to the World of Ducks !** <br>
Experience having a pet duck for the first time, and witness how adorable and charming having one can be! Interact and enjoy a companion you never knew you want--- feed, play, and let it sleep in your care 'cause a pet is what brings joy :>

## Simulation Experience 
```
==============================
Welcome to Pet Duck Simulator!
==============================

Enter the name of your duck: Max

Say hello to your new pet duck, Max!
Take good care of him, Okay? ^v^ 

Max
> Happiness: 30
> Energy: 30
> Hunger: 30
==============================
Interact with your pet duck OvO
[1] Feed
[2] Play
[3] Sleep
[4] Quit & Save
Enter Interaction: 
```
### User Interaction Options
---
**[1] Feed** - take a small piece of bread to ease off the hunger of your pet duck <br>
**[2] Play** - spend quality time to let your pet experience joy and fun <br>
**[3] Sleep** - rest and observe your precious duck recover its strength <br>
**[4] Quit & Save** - grind and take off as your duck waits for your return
